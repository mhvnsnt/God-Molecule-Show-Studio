## Treatment 1 — "HAIR" (Off the Air style: one-word theme episode)

**Theme:** HAIR. The theme is a vibe, not a plot. (Cf. the real S4E1 "Hair" —
see EPISODE-DISSECTIONS.md.)

**Ritual open.** Void. Mars looks left. Looks right. Looks at camera.
"Oh, hey." Beat. "You ever notice how everything you love has hair?
Except soup. Soup is the only honest food." (Koan plants the theme;
Xavier's title-card rule: vary one element — tonight the Void has a
faint snipping sound under it.)

**Arrival.** The forehead symbol dilates — not a glow, an *opening*, like a
mouth deciding. Mars drifts through. Continuous music (one unbroken track
for the whole episode; the transitions are the geography).

**Segment 1 — The Field.** A plain of giant hair strands under a pink sky,
swaying like wheat. Mars narrates like found footage: "This is hair. It
is... doing hair things." The strands sway in perfect rhythm. The joke is
the slope: each shot is slightly closer, slightly louder.

**Segment 2 — The Interview.** A barber's chair the size of a house. The
barber is a wig wearing a smaller wig as a hat. Its duty line (Tong-Nou):
"I cut what grows." Mars asks it the episode's central question: "What is
the body?" The barber answers by lifting its wig, revealing a smaller
barber underneath. No punchline. Cut on the recursion.

**Segment 3 — The Meal.** Kevin appears, mouth full: "Munch, munch. Ahhh,
it's so delicious!" He is eating the landscape. Mars, deadpan: "Kevin.
That's someone's hair." Kevin: "It's *my* hair now. Finders keepers is the
oldest law." Mars considers this. "That's... actually the oldest law."
(Cosmic deadpan: the universe states the joke flatly and moves on.)

**Segment 4 — The Gradient Peak.** Hair brushing hair brushing hair,
faster and faster, scored like a techno crescendo (Sato's land-scoring
model). It doesn't resolve — the music just stops mid-brush. Silence.

**Anti-ending.** Mars back in the Void. Looks left. Looks right. "Anyway."
A single strand of hair floats past. He watches it go. Cut.

**Buried idea (for re-watchers):** the episode is about identity as surface
— everything Mars encounters is defined by what grows on it.

## Treatment 2 — "THE CUSTOMER IS ALWAYS RIGHT" (Xavier style: solution-escalation)

**Cold open (fixed ritual).** Desert. Not the Void — a desert, because this
episode's reality is already wrong from frame one. Mars floats over dunes.
Koan, delivered with total conviction: "They say the customer is always
right. But who is the customer of existence? I am. And I would like to
speak to the manager of reality." Title card flies through frame.

**Premise.** Kevin is hungry. That's the whole problem. "I'm so hungry I
could eat a concept," Kevin says. Mars nods gravely. "Hunger is the body
remembering it was once empty. I will fix this."

**Escalation chain (each fix worse than the last):**
1. Mars manifests a sandwich. It's made of sand. "Desert sandwich. Very
   on-theme." Kevin eats it. His teeth turn to glass.
2. Mars manifests a restaurant. The restaurant is alive and sues him for
   "unauthorized existence." The waiter is a vulture in a bow tie whose duty
   line is: "I serve who waits." They wait. Nothing happens. This goes on
   slightly too long (guilt-free truncation rule: cut *just* before it dies).
3. Mars manifests a farm. The farm grows Kevins. Hundreds of Kevins, all
   hungry, all chanting "MUNCH MUNCH." Mars, to camera: "You see? The problem
   has been diversified." (The Ice-Cream Koan as policy.)
4. Mars manifests the *concept* of food. The concept eats the farm, the
   restaurant, the sandwich, and three dunes. Kevin applauds.

**Anti-ending.** The desert is gone. Mars and Kevin float in the Void.
Kevin: "I'm still hungry." Mars: "Hunger is the body's way of saying the
episode is over." Looks left. Looks right. "Oh, hey." (The ritual
reasserts; nothing was learned; the format is the straight man.)

**Buried idea:** the episode is about help as a form of violence — every
solution is an escalation of the helper's ego.

## Treatment 3 — "THE NINTH LIFE" (Tong-Nou style: death-as-menu life arc)

**Cold open.** Mars dies. Not dramatically — he just... stops, mid-sentence,
in the middle of the Void. "Oh, hey—" Cut to black. A menu appears (the
death menu, Tong-Nou's mechanic): four orifices — eyes, nose, mouth, the
forehead symbol. A cursor hovers. Kevin's voice: "Pick one. You always pick
the forehead. Pick a different one for once." Mars picks the mouth.

**Mission-brief.** Mars wakes inside a new life: he is the moon of a small
angry planet. A stone tablet (his duty line, printed): "YOU ARE THE MOON.
YOUR DUTY: BE LOOKED AT." The planet below is populated by creatures with
telescopes who refuse to look up.

**Quest chain.**
1. Mars tries to be *more* look-at-able: phases, eclipses, a tasteful
   crater arrangement spelling "HEY." The creatures look at their shoes.
2. Kevin arrives (through the forehead portal, which is now a crater).
   Kevin's parallel chaos: he starts a rumor that the moon is made of soup.
   The creatures look up immediately. "Soup?!" (The dumb obvious solution
   works; Mars's philosophy didn't.)
3. The creatures build a giant spoon. They are coming for the moon.
   Mars, deadpan: "I have been looked at. My duty is complete." He lets the
   spoon take him. Death = completion (Retsu's mechanic).

**Ending.** The death menu again. Eight lives remain (the nine-lives arc;
this was life one of the season). Kevin: "Same time next death?" Mars:
"There is no time. There is only the menu." He picks the forehead this time.

**Season spine:** each episode = one life, one duty line, one death that
completes it. The finale reversal (steal from Tong-Nou): the devouring
island/thing the audience feared all season was purifying the soul-fragments.

## Cold opens (3, one per style)

**OTA cold open — "STATIC":** No Void. No "Oh, hey." Just static — 20 seconds
of it, scored like music. Then Mars's voice, flat, from inside the noise:
"This is also an episode." The static resolves into a world. (The format
betrays itself; the audience does the connective work.)

**Xavier cold open — "MORNING":** Desert. Mars floats. Koan: "They say every
morning is a new beginning. I have counted my mornings. There have been
one." Title card flies through his head; he doesn't blink. Kevin walks in
already mid-sentence: "...so that's why you never trust a cactus."

**Tong-Nou cold open — "DUTY":** Black. A single line of text, like a game
manual: "LIFE 4 OF 9. DUTY: TO BE ANNOYED." Mars fades in, already annoyed.
Kevin: "You read that in the manual voice." Mars: "I *am* the manual voice."

## Reality-transition sequences (3)

**1. The datamosh bridge (OTA):** continuous music, no dialogue. The current
reality pixel-sorts into the next one over 8 seconds — teeth become dunes
become teeth. The cut carries the story; never bridge with dialogue
(Bible B law 8).

**2. The organ reaction (Tong-Nou):** the forehead symbol doesn't glow — it
*reacts*. A visitor approaches; the symbol dilates, the dreads lift like
kelp, Mars's eyes track something the audience can't see. The head is a
living island; the portal is an organ. Then: through.

**3. The death-reel (Tong-Nou/Xavier):** Mars dies mid-bit. The death menu
appears (eyes/nose/mouth/forehead). Kevin "re-rolls" the next reality by
picking. The new world loads around the *menu*, which stays visible for one
beat too long — asset-reuse performed as comedy.

## Kevin/Mars bits (4)

**Bit 1 — "FINDERS KEEPERS":**
KEVIN: (mouth full) Finders keepers is the oldest law.
MARS: That's... actually the oldest law. Older than gravity. Gravity had to
find something first.
KEVIN: See? I'm a legal scholar.
MARS: You are the reason laws need keepers.

**Bit 2 — "THE MANUAL":**
MARS: I am the manual voice.
KEVIN: Then read me the troubleshooting section. I'm broken.
MARS: (beat) Have you tried turning yourself off and on again?
KEVIN: I can't. There's no off. There's only *less on*.
MARS: That's the troubleshooting section. You're welcome.

**Bit 3 — "SOUP":**
KEVIN: I'm so hungry I could eat a concept.
MARS: Concepts are high in fiber and low in meaning. Terrible macros.
KEVIN: What about soup?
MARS: Soup is the only honest food. (beat) I don't know what that means
either. I just felt it was true when I said it.

**Bit 4 — "THE ECHO":**
MARS: Life. (the word echoes, per the running-gag architecture)
KEVIN: Why does that echo?
MARS: Everything I say echoes. It's the acoustics of being right.
KEVIN: (whispers) Kevin.
(nothing echoes)
KEVIN: ...rude.
