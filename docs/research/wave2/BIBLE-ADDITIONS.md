## ADDITIONS → BIBLE A, "Canon" tab

### A-C1. The episode skeleton (new subsection: "Episode Construction")
Every episode runs the same machine (all three inspirations agree):
1. **Ritual open:** Void → look left → look right → "Oh, hey." Lock the
   sequence; vary exactly one element per episode (Xavier's title-card rule).
2. **Thesis plant:** Mars delivers one Ice-Cream Koan that secretly names the
   episode's theme.
3. **Arrival:** through the forehead portal into a new reality, filed under
   one of the four lands (Life / Time / Desire / Dreaming) + Central Mountain.
4. **Premise:** one central question from the KEEP-verdicted list, posed
   in-world, never answered cleanly.
5. **Escalation:** Mars applies deadpan wrong philosophy; Kevin runs parallel
   chaos; the world's rules punish wrong assumptions.
6. **Anti-ending:** reset, death-as-transition, or the shrug. No clean
   lessons.
*Source: CRAFT-SYNTHESIS §1; wave-2 episode dissections.*

### A-C2. The four-land elemental taxonomy (new subsection: "Reality Taxonomy")
Realities file under four lands + Central Mountain (Tong-Nou). Each land
locks three axes — **element, music, soul-force**:
- **Life** = Wood · organic/techno-house textures · growth-duty
- **Time** = Fire · driving percussion · urgency-duty
- **Desire** = Metal · sharp synthetic textures · appetite-duty
- **Dreaming** = Water · fluid ambient textures · memory-duty
- **Central Mountain** = the trickster hub (Kevin's territory): barter-only,
  published-but-absurd exchange rates, no money, every trade has a catch.
A reality isn't just themed; it has a locked elemental signature + musical
palette. This is how "almost infinite" stays navigable.
*Source: TONG-NOU-DEEP §3, §7; CRAFT-SYNTHESIS §4.*

### A-C3. Death mechanics (new subsection: "Death as Transition")
Death is a menu, not a punishment (Tong-Nou; Bible B law 9):
1. Progression and inventory *survive* death.
2. The death menu offers re-entry points (eyes / nose / mouth / forehead);
   Kevin "re-rolls" the next reality.
3. A life's mission can complete *by* dying.
4. The only true failure is *refusing* to die — clinging/stasis is the
   unforgivable sin.
*Source: TONG-NOU-DEEP §4; CRAFT-SYNTHESIS §5.*

### A-C4. The nine-lives season arc (new subsection: "Season Structure")
Nine episodes = nine lives = nine soul-fragments of a shattered central
figure, scattered across the four lands and exploited by each land's ruler.
Finale reversal template: the devouring thing the audience feared all season
was the cure. The anthology gets a spine without plotting any single episode.
*Source: TONG-NOU-DEEP §4; CRAFT-SYNTHESIS §5.*

## ADDITIONS → BIBLE A, "Characters" tab

### A-CH1. Mars's voice (new subsection under MARS: "Speech")
- **Native tongue:** the Ice-Cream Koan — correct wisdom-*shape* (koan,
  paradox, aphorism), wrong content. He believes every word. Never winks.
- **Diction:** misfired spiritual/self-help/pseudo-academic forms. Device:
  *hear a phrase → elevate it → break it.*
- **Register:** deadpan folktale — state cosmic facts like a shopkeeper
  states prices. Never explain the weirdness; narrate it as logistics.
- **Conversations:** two monologues colliding, not exchanges. Nobody listens;
  rhythm battles replace dialogue.
- **Running devices:** one meaningless catchphrase-exclamation; one word or
  sound that always echoes in his presence; the forehead symbol intruding
  into scenes like a title card.
*Source: XAVIER-DEEP §1; CRAFT-SYNTHESIS §2, §3.*

### A-CH2. Kevin's voice (new subsection under KEVIN: "Speech")
The counter-rhythm (Off the Air's mixtape logic): if Mars is the deadpan
authority, Kevin is the sensory id — appetite, confusion, the dumb obvious
question. Kevin says what Mars won't. Kevin is parallel chaos, never the
sane foil (the format is the straight man).
*Source: CRAFT-SYNTHESIS §2; TONG-NOU-DEEP §1 (Rin).*

### A-CH3. World-character writing device (new subsection: "NPC Construction")
**"Born with the words of duty":** every world-visitor or soul-fragment ships
with a one-line moral thesis printed on them (e.g. "I fix what bites").
Instant characterization + instant plot generation. Adopted as the show's
character-writing device.
*Source: TONG-NOU-DEEP §1, §6; CRAFT-SYNTHESIS §2.*

## ADDITIONS → BIBLE A, "Ep 1 Staging" tab

### A-E1. Ritual grammar (append to Scene 001)
Adopt Xavier's four-locked-one-varying rule: the Void → look left/right →
"Oh, hey." sequence is locked, but exactly one element varies per episode
(a sound under the Void, a different fourth-wall beat, the koan that plants
the theme). The ritual is what makes the break funny.
*Source: XAVIER-DEEP §1, §6; CRAFT-SYNTHESIS §1.*

### A-E2. Transition language (new subsection: "Reality Transitions")
- **The cut carries the story** (Off the Air): datamosh-style effect bridges
  + continuous music; never bridge with dialogue (Bible B law 8).
- **The organ reaction** (Tong-Nou): the forehead symbol reacts like a living
  organ — dilates, dreads lift — before transit. The head is a living island.
- **The death-reel** (Tong-Nou/Xavier): the death menu stays visible one beat
  too long — asset-reuse performed as comedy.
*Source: OFF-THE-AIR-DEEP §2, §5; TONG-NOU-DEEP §5; wave-2 transitions.*

## ADDITIONS → BIBLE A, "Visual Bible" tab

### A-V1. Deliberate-cheapness standard (append to Materials & Shaders)
Real tools (Blender), stylized limits, never actual corner-cutting. The test:
if the audience can't tell the failure is intentional, it's a bug, not a
style. (Informs Bible B law 5: imperfection may be intentional.)
*Source: XAVIER-DEEP §5 ("the scam"); CRAFT-SYNTHESIS §6.*

### A-V2. Music-first assembly (append to Camera & Grade)
Score the episode before locking visuals — pick the track, then cut to it.
The soundtrack is produced as a standalone album *during* production, tracks
debuting in-episode. Per-land musical palettes (see A-C2).
*Source: OFF-THE-AIR-DEEP §5; TONG-NOU-DEEP §2, §7; CRAFT-SYNTHESIS §6.*

### A-V3. The head reacts (append to Character Colors / World)
Mars's head responds to visitors like a living island (Tong-Nou's head-world
geography): the forehead portal is an organ, not decoration. Clicking /
approaching the head triggers physical reactions.
*Source: TONG-NOU-DEEP §5; CRAFT-SYNTHESIS §4.*

## ADDITIONS → Production pipeline (show repo / production-stage)

### P1. Voice-first pipeline order
Write → **voiceover** → storyboards → animation. The deadpan cadence drives
timing; voices are never fitted to finished animation. Record Mars's voice
before storyboards — pipeline requirement, not preference.
*Source: XAVIER-DEEP §5; CRAFT-SYNTHESIS §6.*

### P2. Commissioning model
Theme + trust, not specs. Per-reality buckets fill over time; when a bucket
fills, it becomes an episode (the serendipity method). Guest curation
parallelizes output.
*Source: OFF-THE-AIR-DEEP §5, §6; CRAFT-SYNTHESIS §6.*

### P3. Dream-journal writer (defined role)
Someone who writes the impossible as mundane — the show's inner-world
writing voice. Separate from the dialogue writer.
*Source: TONG-NOU-DEEP §2; CRAFT-SYNTHESIS §6.*

### P4. The guilt-free truncation rule
Never let a segment overstay — the episode is a mixtape; keep it moving.
When a bit outgrows its container, expand the container — don't trim the bit.
*Source: OFF-THE-AIR-DEEP §6 (Hughes); XAVIER-DEEP §3 (Lee).*
