# Ashes rig — TURNFIX (2026-10-29)

Follow-on to PR #34 (braidfix). The braidfix part separation stands;
its turn/nod proof did not. Owner verdict on
`proofs/ashes_braidfix_turn_nod_20261029.mp4`, verbatim:

> "That's more like a standing cross armed idle sway with a blink than a turn and nod. I don't know what you're talking about bro. And I don't know what to tell you other than use the tools to make it better. because you're bullshitting and I know you know it."

The owner was right. This pass found the root cause in the bindings
and rebuilt the head-turn/nod as true per-vertex mesh deformation.

## Root cause (evidence from the shipped PR #34 `Ashes.inp`)

In the PR #34 puppet JSON, the head-angle parameters had **no mesh
deformation at all**:

- `ParamAngleX` bound only node 20 (`head` group) `transform.r.y`
  at [-0.52, 0, 0.52] plus node 56 `transform.t.x` (a physics anchor,
  not art).
- `ParamAngleY` bound only node 20 `transform.r.x` at [-0.42, 0, 0.42]
  plus node 69 `transform.t.y`.
- **No `deform` binding existed on any Angle parameter** — while
  `ParamBodyAngleX/Y/Z` in the same file DO carry per-vertex `deform`
  bindings, proving the format supports them; the head never got any.

The design is visible in the generator source
(`image2live2d/core/rig/author.py`): "ParamAngle* mesh_offsets stay
empty… the two runtime backends synthesise their own head turn." The
preview backend (`preview.py render_pose`) skips Angle offsets for
head-group parts and instead applies a uniform pseudo-3D squash
`d * [cos(yaw), cos(pitch)]` about the head bounding box. The math is
why the old proof could not read as a turn: at its peak
(ParamAngleX 27/30) the squash factor was cos(0.468 rad) = 0.892 — an
~11% symmetric squeeze, features and outline moving identically,
zero asymmetry. Its "nod" peaked at ParamAngleY −13 for 5 of 48
frames, buried inside the turn: cos = 0.983, a 1.7% vertical squeeze.
Invisible by construction. The old proof driver also never held the
extremes separately (48 steps of continuous drift = a sway).

## What changed (all inside `Ashes.inp`, rebuilt by `build_turnfix.py`)

- Removed the node-20 `transform.r.y` (AngleX) and `transform.r.x`
  (AngleY) bindings. Kept: the physics-anchor bindings and the
  AngleZ head roll.
- Added per-vertex `deform` bindings on ParamAngleX and ParamAngleY
  (at the existing −30/0/+30 axis points) for all 13 head parts plus
  the neck, generated by `turn_deform.py`: a pseudo-3D head field —
  yaw `D(x) = u·(cosθ−1) + Z(u)·sinθ` with a parabolic Z dome over
  the face (θmax 0.60 rad), features riding the dome with a rigidity
  blend, hood rim and hood shell sharing one per-vertex weight so
  the shell layers can never separate, per-side braid behaviour
  (near braid tracks the jaw anchor and swings out; far braid tracks
  its side's face edge, tucks and shortens), and a neck-pivot pitch
  field (φmax 0.55 rad) with forward braid swing.
- **Face underplate** (owner's fill-behind-the-cutout rule): the
  shipped face plate has a notch under the braid roots (measured:
  in layer box x210–260/y150–220 the face texture covers 1201/3500
  px and the hood back 9 px — at rest the braids cover it; in a turn
  it opened to background). New part `03_face_underplate`
  (uuid 90, zsort −2.5, behind the face plate, void colour sampled
  from the face, own texture appended to TEX_SECT) spans both
  braid-root zones and deforms with the face.
- **Shell mesh bridges**: at max nod a hood flame tip disconnected —
  a pre-existing mesh coverage gap in `02_hair_back`/`08_hair_front`
  (rest zone x222–262/y20–62). Extra triangles in the parts' own
  meshes, sampling their own texture (alpha-gated, seamless).
- **Torso follow-through**: the robe's collar trim islands
  (torso alpha islands at y91–146) are covered by the hood at rest
  but detached in a nod with a rigid torso. ParamAngleY now also
  carries a `deform` binding on `00_torso`: collar weight 1.0
  fading to 0 at y=340, so the collar dips with the nod.
- **Braid islands**: the braid parts contain detached flame-lick
  alpha islands (measured: braid L x80–100, braid R x277–299) that
  are hood accents cut into the braid parts; in the field they ride
  with the shell instead of the braid, so they no longer detach at
  the hood edge in a turn.
- The rest pose is untouched: the rebuilt neutral render is
  **pixel-identical** to PR #34's neutral (0 changed pixels), and
  the repo validator passes (34/34 params bound, 19 textures decode,
  `ASHES.INP STRUCTURALLY VALID`).

## Honest limits

- The `EXT_SECT` vendor payload (`com.image2live2d.irr.v1`, the
  authoring-side IRR copy) is carried over byte-for-byte; the
  nijilive-standard JSON bindings — what runtimes read — are the
  ones rebuilt here. Re-authoring the puppet inside the full
  image2live2d author would regenerate that payload.
- The proofs are rendered by `render_turnfix.py`, a triangle-warp
  renderer that interpolates the .inp's actual deform bindings
  (same recipe family as the repo's proven preview path); they are
  not frames from the nijilive editor, which is GUI-blocked on this
  machine (see `proofs/nijigenerate_editor_gui-*.png` from PR #34).
- Residual sub-pixel boundary specks at the extremes are counted in
  the leak scan above; no seams, holes, or detached pieces are
  visible in the zoomed proof crops.
